# Validate AR Case

Validate `runs/<case_id>` without changing measurement values. Check every agent result JSON, schema conformance, quantitative measurement, unit, calibration source, frame/source provenance, artifact path, mask, contour, measurement line, spectral envelope, threshold application, guideline provenance, and safety statement.

Reject final reports that lack required quantitative AR measurements or inspectable artifacts. Open concrete repair tasks for unsupported conclusions, missing calibration provenance, rejected artifacts, or invalid units; do not convert those defects into a global not-enough-information result for complete exams.
