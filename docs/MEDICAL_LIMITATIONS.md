# Medical Limitations

This system is research and decision-support development infrastructure. It is not an autonomous diagnostic device. Synthetic fixtures do not provide clinical validation.

For the provided complete high-quality exam set, missing calibration, rejected artifacts, unsupported medical rules, or failed extraction are engineering/tooling defects to repair and audit before final reporting. Discordance and weak context-sensitive metrics must be preserved with quality flags and reviewer notes; they must not be averaged away or converted into invented measurements.
